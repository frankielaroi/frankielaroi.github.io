Now: keeping batteries moving at Kofa.

35+ stories, tasks and fixes in my first two months, across the operations dashboard, network platform, fleet-partner portal and Flutter rider app. Scroll to walk through a station.

1 of 4

Alerts that reach people

I built a real-time notification inbox and browser push alerts (service worker, web push). Operations staff hear about at-risk stations and new battery-movement requests even with the app closed, so supply problems get handled sooner.

2 of 4

Manual slot unlock

When a station slot faults, riders on battery-redistribution trips can still unlock it by hand and finish the pickup or drop-off in the Flutter rider app. Batteries keep moving and stay correctly counted instead of trips failing.

3 of 4

Slot activity log

A slot-level log with search and type and date filters. Field technicians and customer support diagnose a faulty slot in one place instead of digging through the whole station's history.

4 of 4

Live fleet map

Fleet partners get live moving, parked and immobilised bike counts on their network map. I also fixed the data bugs (battery counts between platforms, slot status after removal) that were giving operations teams wrong numbers.
