Questions I get asked.

What kind of work do you take on?

Full stack and frontend engineering: React and Next.js interfaces, Node.js, NestJS, Laravel and FastAPI backends, Flutter mobile apps, and database and API design. I have also built complete platforms alone when a client needed the whole thing.

Are you available for remote work?

Yes. I'm in Accra on GMT and have worked remotely with distributed teams at Reuben Foundation Africa, Hepeps IT Solutions and AfRipul Group. On-site in Accra works too.

How long will my project take?

It depends on scope. A focused frontend build is usually weeks; a full platform with backend, database and deployment takes longer. Send the requirements and I'll give you a realistic estimate rather than an optimistic one.

Do you support what you build after launch?

Yes. I ship with CI/CD and metrics in place so problems are visible, and I'm happy to arrange ongoing maintenance or hand over with documentation.
